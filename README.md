## 🇺🇸 English

### Overview
This repository contains the TAVARDT official logic for scaling paid media campaigns. In high-ticket B2B markets, simple ROI calculations are not enough. Our intelligence model utilizes diminishing returns coefficients to predict how your campaigns will perform as you increase budget, ensuring profitable growth without efficiency leakage.

### Key Features
* ROI & ROAS Auditing: Accurate calculation of your current performance metrics.
* Scale Projection: Mathematical modeling of budget increases and performance decay.
* Efficiency Loss Analysis: Identifying the exact point where budget scaling becomes unprofitable.
* B2B Focused: Optimized for long sales cycles and complex lead generation funnels.

### Usage
Include the calculator.js utility in your project:
```javascript
const projection = ROI_Calculator.projectScale(1000, 5000, 5.0);
```
